Cumin

Also: jeera

FormWhole/ground
Whole life1 yr
Ground life3 mo
Bloom time10 sec

Quick take

Good for

  • ✓ Gut-friendly (jeera water)
  • ✓ Warms any savoury dish
  • ✓ Whole seeds keep 1 year
  • ✓ Iron-rich, cheap

Watch out

  • ✗ Pre-ground goes flat fast
  • ✗ Burnt cumin turns bitter (10 sec too long)
  • ✗ Easy to confuse with caraway visually
  • ✗ Overpowers if overused
