Second ferment

Herb-infused kombucha.

Subtle, grown-up, aromatic. Mint · basil · tulsi · lavender · rosemary.

Ferment1–2 days
Add5 leaves
FizzGentle
SkillTodo •

Do this

1

Bruise the leaves

Clap between hands to release oils. Don't tear — bitter.

2

Add to bottle with 1 tsp honey

Herbs alone won't fizz — the honey is the fizz food.

3

Top with base, cap, wait

1–2 days at room temp. Burp daily.

4

Fridge, strain, serve

Strain out spent leaves. Ice.
