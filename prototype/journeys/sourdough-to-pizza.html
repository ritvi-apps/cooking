Starter → pizza

Tangy, chewy, blistered.

Cold-fermented sourdough crust. Mix on day 1, bake on day 2 or 3. Better than any yeasted dough.

Makes4 pies
Cold rise24–72 hr
Bake6 min
SkillTodo •

The idea

1

Mix cool

500 g bread flour + 350 g cold water + 100 g starter + 12 g salt + 20 g olive oil.

2

Bulk 4 hr room temp

4 sets of stretch & fold in the first 2 hr. Then rest.

3

Divide, ball, fridge

4 balls. Cold retard 24–72 hr. Longer = tangier.

3-day fridge = magic crust
4

Bake hottest possible

Preheated cast iron or steel, oven at max. Stretch cold dough, top fast, bake 5–7 min.
