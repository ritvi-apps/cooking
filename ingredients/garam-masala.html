Spice · blend

Garam masala

Literally: "warming spice mix"

FormBlend
Life3 mo
Add atThe end
Dose½–1 tsp

Quick take

Good for

  • ✓ Cardamom + clove + cinnamon + pepper in one
  • ✓ "Restaurant finish" trivial
  • ✓ Warming — cold-weather friend
  • ✓ Home blends beat any store

Watch out

  • ✗ Store-bought often flat
  • ✗ Overuse dominates a dish
  • ✗ Bitter if added early under long simmer
  • ✗ Every brand tastes different

Make it in 5 minutes

Dry-toast then grind: 1 tbsp cumin · 1 tbsp coriander seed · 4 cloves · 6 green cardamom pods · 1 inch cinnamon · ½ tsp black pepper.

Store in an airtight jar, dark cupboard.
