Quick take

Good for

  • ✓ Universal flavour amplifier
  • ✓ Real antimicrobial + heart benefits
  • ✓ Transforms at every heat level
  • ✓ Cheap, months of pantry life

Watch out

  • ✗ Burnt garlic → unfixable bitter
  • ✗ Not sattvic (Jain / some yogic diets)
  • ✗ Raw + empty stomach = acidity
  • ✗ Cut the green shoot in sprouted cloves
