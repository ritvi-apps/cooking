Quick take

Good for

  • ✓ High-protein vegetarian meals
  • ✓ Grilling, tikka, curries
  • ✓ Absorbs marinades cleanly
  • ✓ Home-made > store

Watch out

  • ✗ Squeaky if boiled > 3 min
  • ✗ Not vegan
  • ✗ Higher saturated fat than tofu
  • ✗ Store-bought is often rubbery

Goes well with

Greens

🥬 spinach 🌿 methi 🥗 mustard greens

Aromatics & spice

🫚 ginger 🧄 garlic 🌰 cumin 🍂 garam masala

Finishers

🌿 kasuri methi 🍋 lemon 🧈 butter

Keep it fresh

🛒

Buy

Firm, off-white, no yellowing. Avoid "malai".

Submerge in water. Change daily. 4–5 days.

🧊

Cube, tray-freeze, bag. 2 mo. Curries only.
