Palak paneer

Bright spinach gravy, cubed paneer, no cream. The version I actually make on a Tuesday, not the restaurant heavy one.

Serves4
Prep15 min
Cook30 min
DifficultyEasy

What you need

🧀

Paneer

250 g · cubed

→ 🥬

Spinach

500 g · fresh

→ 🫚

Ginger

1 inch

→ 🧄

Garlic

4 cloves

→ 🌰

Cumin seeds

1 tsp

→ 🍂

Garam masala

1 tsp

→

Plus: 1 onion, 1 tomato, 1 green chilli, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp kasuri methi, salt, 2 tbsp ghee, 1 tbsp butter.

Do this

1

Blanch the spinach 💧

Salted boiling water → 45 sec → ice bath. Locks the green.

⚠ don't skip the shock
2

Blend cold, keep it coarse 🌀

Spinach + green chilli + half the ginger-garlic + 2 tbsp water. 10 sec pulse only.

3

Bloom cumin in ghee 🔥

Cumin crackles → add onion. Cook translucent, not brown.

4

Ginger-garlic + tomato 🍅

Cook until fat pools at the edges. That's the "masala ready" cue.

5

Kill the heat before adding spinach 🛑

Spinach loves low heat. Add blend, stir, turn back to low, simmer 4–5 min.

🎯 this is the recipe
6

Fold in paneer 🧀

Gently. Simmer 2 min max — longer turns it squeaky.

7

Finish, rest, serve 🍽

Kasuri methi (crushed), garam masala, a knob of butter. Off heat. Rest 5 min.

Notes for future me

💡

Blend cold, cook low

First time I blended spinach hot → army-green sludge. Cold blend + low heat = bright and grassy.

🚫

Skip the cream

Restaurant habit. At home the ghee + butter finish is enough.

📦

Meal-prep tip

Gravy holds 3 days. Add paneer only when reheating — pre-cooked paneer turns rubbery.

Try it different

🌱

Palak tofu

Straight 1:1 sub for vegan. Press the tofu.

🌽

Palak corn

Add sweet corn in step 6. Kid-friendly.

🍶

Restaurant-y

2 tbsp cream in step 7. Heavier finish.
