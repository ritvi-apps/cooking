Second ferment

Fruit kombucha, fizzy.

Base kombucha + a bit of fruit → sealed → 1–3 days at room temp = fizz.

Ferment1–3 days
Add10% fruit
DifficultyEasy
SkillTodo •

Do this

1

Fruit in the bottle first

2 tbsp fresh fruit purée or juice per 500 ml bottle. Strawberry, mango, apple, pomegranate work well.

2

Top with base 'buch

Leave 2 cm headroom. Cap tight.

3

Wait 1–3 days at room temp

Burp bottles daily (release CO₂) for the first two days.

skip burping = bottle bomb
4

Fridge → serve cold

Chilling stops the fermentation. Now you have fizzy fruit 'buch.
