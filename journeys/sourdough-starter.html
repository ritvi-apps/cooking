Grow a sourdough starter.

Flour + water + wild yeast in the air. Feed daily. In 5–7 days, it'll be strong enough to raise bread.

room temp

🫙

Glass jar

Do this — one week

1

Day 1 · mix and wait

50 g flour + 50 g water in the jar. Stir. Cover loosely. Warm spot. Do nothing for 24 hours.

2 min
2

Day 2–3 · discard + feed

Discard half. Add 50 g flour + 50 g water. Stir. Wait 24 hours. May look boring — that's fine.

false rise likely — ignore it
3

Day 4–6 · twice-daily feeds

Morning and evening: discard half → feed 50 g / 50 g. It'll start bubbling and rising between feeds.

4

Day 5–7 · float test

Drop a spoonful into water. Floats = ready to bake bread. Sinks = feed one more day.

float = green light
5

Maintain forever

Baking often: feed daily on the counter. Baking weekly: fridge, feed once a week. Give it a name.
