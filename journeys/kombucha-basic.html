First ferment

Brew the base kombucha.

Sweet tea + SCOBY + starter liquid. Sit for 7–14 days. That's a whole brew.

Batch2 L
Ferment7–14 days
Hands-on20 min
SkillTodo •

What you need

🍵

Black tea

6 bags · 2 L water

🍯

White sugar

200 g · standard

🫙

SCOBY + starter

from a friend or shop

Do this

1

Brew strong sweet tea

Boil 2 L water → steep 6 bags of black tea 10 min → stir in 200 g sugar → cool completely.

hot tea kills the SCOBY
2

Pour into a clean jar

Wide-mouth glass. Never metal touching the SCOBY.

3

Add starter liquid + SCOBY

200 ml of previous batch (or store-bought raw kombucha) + the SCOBY. It's fine if it sinks.

4

Cover with cloth, sit

Breathable cloth + rubber band. Warm spot (~22–26 °C). Dark corner. No fridge.

5

Taste from day 7

Should taste tangy but still slightly sweet. Longer = more vinegary. Stop when you like it.

taste = the only ready signal
6

Reserve, bottle

Save 200 ml + SCOBY for the next batch. The rest goes into bottles → second ferment.
