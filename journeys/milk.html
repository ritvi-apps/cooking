Four things I can make from milk

🧀
Paneer solid
Split milk with acid → drain → press. The vegetarian workhorse protein.
⏱ 30 min easy 1 recipe uses it
→
🍶
Curd (dahi) learning
Warm milk + a spoon of yesterday's curd → wait overnight.
⏱ 6–10 hr easy → lassi, raita, kadhi
→
🧈
Butter (makhan) todo
Skim cream from milk → churn or blend until it splits.
⏱ 20 min easy → ghee, buttermilk
→
🍮
Khoya (mawa) todo
Reduce milk on low heat until it's a soft dough. Base of most Indian sweets.
⏱ 2 hr medium → gulab jamun, barfi
→

The one idea

Milk is protein floating in water. Every transformation is a different way of making the protein clump — acid, culture, heat, churn, or long reduction.
