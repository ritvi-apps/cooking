Milk → khoya (mawa)

Reduce milk to a soft dough.

Slow simmer for 2 hours, stir constantly at the end. The base of most Indian sweets.

Time~2 hr
Yield1L → 200g
DifficultyMedium
SkillTodo •

The idea

1

Wide, heavy pan 🍳

More surface area = faster reduction. Kadhai works.

2

Simmer, stir often 🌀

Medium-low. Scrape the sides — that browning is flavour.

3

Last 20 min: constant stir 🥄

Now it can scorch. Do not walk away.

4

Dough consistency = done 🎯

Pulls away from the pan into a soft, pale-brown ball.
