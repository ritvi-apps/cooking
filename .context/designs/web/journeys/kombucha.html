Kombucha · journey

Fizzy fermented tea.

Sweet tea + a jelly-like SCOBY = tangy fizzy drink in 7–14 days. Endless flavor variations in the second ferment.

The one idea

The SCOBY eats sugar and produces acid + a little alcohol + fizz. Flavor is added after the SCOBY does its work, in the second ferment.
