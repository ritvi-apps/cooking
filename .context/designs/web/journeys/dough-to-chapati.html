Dough → chapati

Soft chapati that puffs.

Whole wheat, water, salt — that's it. The puff comes from technique, not ingredients.

Makes8 rotis
Mix10 min
Cook3 min each
SkillLearning ⌛

What you need

🌾

Atta

2 cups whole wheat

💧

Warm water

~¾ cup

🧂

Salt · ghee

½ tsp · 1 tsp

Do this

1

Mix & shaggy

Atta + salt + a tsp of ghee. Add water bit by bit until it just holds.

3 min
2

Knead until smooth

Push and fold for 5 min. Soft, smooth, springs back when poked.

softer than you think
3

Rest, covered

20 min. Gluten relaxes → thin rolls without tearing.

20 min
4

Divide, dust, roll

8 balls. Dust with dry atta. Roll thin — turn quarter-turn between passes for a round shape.

5

Hot dry tawa

Medium-high. Slap the roti on. 20 seconds → flip → 30 seconds → tiny bubbles appear.

6

Flame puff (the magic)

Tongs, straight over gas flame. It balloons in 5 seconds. Flip once. Done.

the puff = the reward
7

Ghee brush, stack

Brush with ghee. Stack in a lined casserole → stays soft for hours.

If something goes wrong

Didn't puff

Rolled uneven, or tawa too cold. Both fix themselves next time.

Cracked at edges

Dough too dry → knead in a few drops of water and rest again.

Turned hard/leathery

Cooked too long on the tawa. 30 seconds each side is enough before the flame.
