Discard → breakfast

Never throw discard out again.

Tangy, tender pancakes in 15 minutes. Uses the 100 g of starter you'd normally toss.

Makes6
Time15 min
DifficultyEasy
SkillTodo •

Do this

1

Whisk it all together

200 g discard + 200 g milk + 1 egg + 2 tbsp sugar + ½ tsp salt + ½ tsp baking soda + 30 g melted butter.

2

Rest 5 min

Soda + acid start fizzing. The bubbles are the puff.

3

Butter, ladle, flip

Medium heat. Flip when bubbles pop across the surface and edges look dry.

4

Butter, maple, done

Also great with jaggery + ghee, or a spoonful of dahi.
