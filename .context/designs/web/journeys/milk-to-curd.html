Milk → curd (dahi)

Set curd overnight.

Warm milk + a spoon of live culture + a warm spot to sleep. That's it.

Time6–10 hr
Hands-on5 min
DifficultyEasy
SkillLearning ⌛

What you need

🥛

Whole milk

500 ml

🥄

Live curd

1 tbsp starter

🌡

Warm spot

~30 °C

Do this

1

Warm the milk 🔥

Bring just to a boil. Kill the heat.

8 min
2

Cool to lukewarm 🌡

Finger test: comfortable, not hot. Too hot kills the culture.

10 min⚠ this is the trick
3

Whisk in the starter 🥄

1 tbsp of yesterday's curd. Stir smooth.

1 min
4

Cover, warm, wait 🛌

Oven with light on, warm cupboard, wrapped in a towel. Summer: 6 hr. Winter: 10 hr.

6–10 hr
5

Fridge → set fully 🧊

2 more hours in the fridge finishes the set. Now it slices clean.

2 hr

If something goes wrong

💧

Watery / not set

Milk was too hot when starter went in, or spot wasn't warm enough. Try again.

😖

Too sour

Left it out too long. Refrigerate earlier next time.
