Butter → ghee

Golden, nutty, shelf-stable.

Simmer butter → water evaporates → milk solids brown → strain the gold.

Time25 min
Yield~80%
DifficultyEasy
SkillTodo •

The idea

1

Melt butter on low 🔥

Heavy-bottomed pan. No stirring.

2

Foam rises → skim (optional) 🥄

Some traditions skim, some don't. Either works.

3

Wait for the crackle to quiet 🔉

Water is out. Milk solids drop to the bottom and start browning.

4

Golden = done ✨

Amber liquid, nutty smell, brown flecks at the bottom. Kill the heat.

5

Strain through muslin 💧

Into a clean, dry glass jar. Shelf-stable for months.
