Milk → paneer

Make paneer in 30 minutes.

Split milk with an acid, drain, press. That's the whole thing.

Time30 min
Yield~200 g
DifficultyEasy
SkillSolid ✓

What you need

🥛

Whole milk

1 litre · full fat

🍋

Lemon

2 tbsp juice

🧻

Muslin cloth

+ colander, weight

Do this

1

Boil the milk 🔥

Medium heat. Big lazy bubbles = ready.

~10 min 🥄 stir often
2

Kill the heat, wait 30 seconds ⏸

The "shivering point." Hot but not raging.

30 sec
3

Add lemon, one tbsp at a time 🍋

Stir gently. Stop when the whey turns clear yellow-green.

2 min ⚠ don't dump it all in
4

Rest 5 minutes 🧘

Curds firm up. Don't stir.

5 min
5

Strain through muslin 💧

Save the whey — knead into roti dough, water your plants.

2 min ♻ save the whey
6

Rinse with cold water 🚿

Washes off residual acid. Skip this → sour paneer.

1 min
7

Twist, press, wait ⚖

Cubes → 30 min under 2 kg. Grilling → 2 hours. Bhurji → skip pressing.

30 min – 2 hr choose your press

Choose your acid

🍋

Lemon

Softest texture. Slight citrus note. My default.

🧪

White vinegar

Firmer. Cleaner flavour. Best for grilling.

♻

Old whey

Traditional. Softer curds. Zero waste.

If something goes wrong

😰

Milk didn't split

Add another tablespoon of acid. If it still won't budge → milk is ultra-pasteurized; try a fresher brand next time.

🥶

Paneer is rubbery

You skipped the rinse or pressed too hard. Ease off next time.

😖

Tastes sour

Too much acid. Rinse the curds better and stop the moment whey turns clear.
