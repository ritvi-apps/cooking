Second ferment

Spicy ginger fizz.

The gut-shot classic — potent, gingery, aggressive fizz. Small bottles work best.

Ferment2 days
Add1 tbsp/bottle
FizzVery
SkillTodo •

Do this

1

Grate fresh ginger

1 tbsp finely grated per 500 ml bottle. Fresh only — dried won't ferment.

2

Optional: 1 tsp honey

Extra sugar = extra fizz. Skip if base is already sweet enough.

3

Top, cap, burp daily

Ginger produces aggressive fizz. Burp every 12 hours.

burp twice a day, not once
4

Fridge day 2, strain, serve

Strain out the ginger fibers as you pour. Ice cold.
