Starter → bread

The 24-hour loaf.

Mix at night, fold morning, bake evening. Crisp crust, open crumb, tangy.

Makes1 boule
Timeline~24 hr
Hands-on40 min
SkillTodo •

The rhythm

1

Evening · mix + rest

500 g bread flour + 350 g water + 100 g active starter + 10 g salt. Mix. Rest 30 min (autolyse).

2

Evening · stretch & fold ×4

Every 30 min for 2 hours. Grab, stretch up, fold over. Turn bowl. Four times per set.

3

Overnight · bulk ferment

Cool room (~20 °C) for 8–10 hr, OR warm room 4–6 hr. Doubles in size.

4

Morning · shape + cold retard

Pre-shape → 20 min bench rest → final shape → banneton → fridge 4–24 hr.

cold retard = flavor + easier scoring
5

Evening · Dutch oven, hot

Preheat DO to 240 °C for 45 min. Score dough. Bake covered 25 min → uncovered 15 min.

6

Cool completely

2 hours on a rack. Cutting hot = gummy. This is the hardest step.

Full write-up after the first loaf that survives being cut.
