Dough → paratha

Layered, flaky, weekend bread.

Same dough as chapati. Fold ghee into the layers, roll flat, shallow-fry.

Makes6
Cook5 min each
DifficultyMedium
SkillTodo •

Do this

1

Roll a chapati

Same dough, same first roll.

2

Brush with ghee, fold triangle

Half → quarter fold with ghee at each fold. Ghee = the flakes.

3

Roll triangle out flat

Keep it triangular. Dust with atta if it sticks.

4

Tawa + ghee on top

Medium heat. Flip when bubbly. Drizzle ghee → press with a cloth for even golden.
