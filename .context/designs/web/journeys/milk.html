Four things I can make from milk

Paneersolid
Split milk with acid → drain → press.
30 min 1 recipe uses it
🍶
Curd (dahi)learning
Warm milk + starter → wait overnight.
6–10 hr → lassi · raita · kadhi
🧈
Butter (makhan)todo
Skim cream → churn until it splits.
20 min → ghee · buttermilk
🍮
Khoya (mawa)todo
Reduce milk on low heat to a soft dough.
2 hr → gulab jamun · barfi

The one idea

Milk is protein floating in water. Every transformation is a different way of making the protein clump — acid, culture, heat, churn, or long reduction.
