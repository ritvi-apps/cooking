Tomato

9 great matches · 6 to avoid · open full dossier →

Pairs beautifully with

🌿
Basil
Both share eugenol and linalool. Their oils intensify each other — the caprese is chemistry, not just tradition.
🫒
Olive oil
Dissolves lycopene for the body (4× absorption). Coats acid so it doesn't sting. Oil before salt.
🧄
Garlic
Sulfur compounds balance the sweetness. Slow, low heat — never brown, or bitterness ruins the sauce.
🧀
Mozzarella
Fat softens the acid on your tongue. Milk protein binds lycopene for a slower, steadier release.
🧅
Onion
Cooked slow, onion sugars caramelize and mellow tomato's edge. The base of nearly every world sauce.
🥚
Eggs
Yolk fat carries lycopene. Acid tightens egg proteins — shakshuka, tomato omelette, huevos rancheros.
🫘
Chickpea
Vitamin C in tomato boosts iron absorption from the chickpea by ~3×. Chana masala isn't an accident.
🌶️
Cumin
Earthy anchor for tomato's bright top note. The reason Mexican and Indian sauces feel familiar.
🧂
Salt (at rest)
Draws out water from cut tomato. 15 min salted → concentrated flavor, better texture, less watery salad.

Avoid or handle carefully

🥒
Cucumber (raw, at length)
Cucumber has ascorbinase — an enzyme that destroys tomato's vitamin C. Fine in a small salad; not for juice.
🥛
Milk (raw, cold)
Acid curdles cold milk instantly. Cream in a hot sauce is different — heat and emulsion stabilize casein.
🥔
Potato (heavy stacking)
Both are nightshades. Loading the same meal doubles the alkaloid dose if you're sensitive.
🍋
Extra citrus (raw dish)
Tomato is already 4% acid. More lemon flattens its own tartness. Save citrus for cooked dishes that lost brightness.
🥫
Aluminum cookware
Acid leaches metal. Off-flavor, off-color. Stainless, enamel, or seasoned cast iron only.
🥶
Whole fruit in the fridge
Below 12 °C, aroma enzymes shut off permanently. Flavor doesn't return with warming.

Traditions that work for a reason

Classic pairings

Turmeric + fat

Curcumin is fat-soluble. Ghee, coconut oil, or full-fat milk raises absorption 7–10×. Never add turmeric to a fat-free dish.

Turmeric + black pepper

Piperine in pepper inhibits the liver enzyme that clears curcumin. Bioavailability rises up to 2000%. This is why the two live together in every masala.

🍋

Iron greens + vitamin C

Non-heme iron (spinach, lentils) absorbs 3× better with 25 mg vitamin C at the same meal. Squeeze lemon on the dal.

🍚
+
🫘

Rice + legume

Rice is low in lysine; legumes are low in methionine. Together they cover all nine essential amino acids. Every ancient food culture found this independently.

🥑
+
🥕

Carotenoid + fat

Beta-carotene (carrot, sweet potato) needs fat to absorb. Salad with avocado delivers 4–8× the carotenoids of a fat-free one.

+
🍯

Sourdough + honey

Fermentation lowers gluten-related irritation and predigests carbs. Honey has enzymes that gut bacteria feed on. Both are gentle on the microbiome.

Common mistakes

Combinations to skip

Milk + citrus
Citric acid drops milk's pH below the casein isoelectric point (~4.6). Proteins clump instantly. Add citrus after heat has stabilized proteins in a cooked dish.
Spinach + dairy (for iron)
Calcium binds oxalates in spinach. You lose most of the iron you thought you were getting. If the goal is iron, eat greens with vitamin C, not paneer.
Tea + iron-rich meal
Tannins in tea block ~60% of non-heme iron absorption. Wait one hour after a lentil or spinach dish before your chai.
Honey heated above 40 °C
Ayurveda names this a "toxic" combination; food science agrees on the smaller point — enzymes and delicate aromatics degrade above ~40 °C. Stir into warm, not hot.
