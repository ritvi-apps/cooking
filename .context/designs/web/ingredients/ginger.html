Also: adrak

CategoryAromatic

Quick take

Good for

  • ✓ Digestion, anti-nausea
  • ✓ Long fridge life; freezes perfectly
  • ✓ Heat + citrus + earth in one
  • ✓ Foundational to every gravy

Watch out

  • ✗ Old ginger goes fibrous
  • ✗ Raw at the end overpowers
  • ✗ Blood-thinner interactions in huge doses

Goes well with
