Vegetable · leafy green

Spinach

Also: palak

CategoryLeafy
PeakWinter
Fridge3–4 days
Shrinks~85%

Quick take

Good for

  • ✓ Iron, folate, vitamin K
  • ✓ Cooks in under a minute
  • ✓ Blends into gravies neatly
  • ✓ Cheap, widely available

Watch out

  • ✗ High oxalates (kidney stones)
  • ✗ Turns sludge-green if overheated
  • ✗ Shrinks a LOT — buy extra
  • ✗ Wilts fast — use same-day

Goes well with

Proteins

🧀 paneer 🌱 tofu 🫘 chickpeas

Aromatics & spice

🫚 ginger 🧄 garlic 🌰 cumin 🌶 green chilli

Keep it fresh

🛒

Buy

Bright, crisp stems. No yellow or slime.

❄

Fridge

Paper towel in a bag. Don't wash until use.

🧊

Freezer

Blanch, shock, squeeze, portion. Ready anytime.
