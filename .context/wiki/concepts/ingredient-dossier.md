## Summary
A dossier is everything about one ingredient on one page.

## Why it matters
**It replaced the journey as the unit of the site.** On 2026-07-19 the site
moved from a transformation graph to a dossier per ingredient, with the journey
as one chapter of it.

**The set draws three shapes of it, and they disagree.**

| Shape | Where | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| Seven chapters | Home describes it | Origin, Grow, Source, Store, Prep & Cook, Pairs / Avoid, In the body |
| Bento, one frame | Tomato | Grow, Source, Storage, Cook, Pairs with / Avoid with |
| Older page | Paneer and five more | Quick take, Goes well with, If you're out, Keep it fresh, Used in |

## Implications
- Tomato is the reference. The other six are to be redrawn like it.
- Origin and In the body were dropped from the bento.
- Home still says "7 chapters" on every tile.
- Milk, Wheat and Sourdough have a journey and no dossier.
