# Purpose

What hamburg is for, who it is for, and what it is not. From the repo's own pages.
The repository is named `cooking`; the product is hamburg.

## What it is

A kitchen companion: for one ingredient, what it is, what it can become and what
it pairs with ([map-not-manual](map-not-manual.md)). The site is built from
ingredient dossiers ([dossiers](../decisions/dossiers.md)). It answers
[PROB-0001](../../problems/0001-kitchen-know-how-is-forgotten-between-cooks.md):
dishes that were cooked and liked get forgotten, with their steps.

## Who it is for

One person, the owner, who is the only reader today
([PROB-0001](../../problems/0001-kitchen-know-how-is-forgotten-between-cooks.md)).

## What it is not

- Not a recipe manual: a map of what an ingredient can become ([map-not-manual](map-not-manual.md)).
- Not for meat or fish: everything is vegetarian ([vegetarian-only](vegetarian-only.md)).
- Not an app: the repo holds no application code, only content and a design set.

## Status

Designs only. The site is not served anywhere yet, so it cannot be opened at the
stove ([PROB-0002](../../problems/0002-the-site-cannot-be-opened-while-cooking.md)).
The cookbook holds one recipe. Words used here: [glossary](glossary.md).
