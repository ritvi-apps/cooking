## Summary
Every technique is in one of three states: solid, learning or todo.

## Why it matters
**The site tracks what its owner can do, not only what exists.** Skills says a
skill is solid when it can be produced from memory in a distracted kitchen.

## Implications
- Solid is sage, learning is butter, todo is grey. The journey graphs carry
  the same three dots.
- One skill is solid: milk to paneer. Two are learning: milk to curd, and
  tempering.
- A journey card carries the state of the journey as a whole.
