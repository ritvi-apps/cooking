## Why it is drawn this way
**Converted, not redrawn.** This is the prototype's own markup, with its nav bar, footer and icons written in on 2026-10-04. See [[converted-design-set]].

**The page is a graph, and every node is a link.** The ingredient sits on the left and each thing it becomes is a node to the right. See [[map-not-manual]].

**The graph is an inline drawing.** Its geometry is written out in this file, not declared once in `shared.css`.
