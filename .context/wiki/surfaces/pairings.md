## Why it is drawn this way
**Converted, not redrawn.** This is the prototype's own markup, with its nav bar, footer and icons written in on 2026-10-04. See [[converted-design-set]].

**Every pairing has a reason.** Flavour chemistry, nutrient absorption, or a tradition that lasted because it worked. A pairing with no reason does not go on the page.

**Only Tomato is drawn.** The picker shows six ingredients and says 6 of 40; four of them link to #, and the picker does nothing.

**Coral means avoid.**
