## Summary
From what is on the counter to a dish: the main path of the flow chart.

## Audience
end-user

## Entry
Home. There is no splash and no sign-in.

## Steps
| # | Step | Surface | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home | [[home]] | Six ingredient tiles |
| 2 | Ingredients | [[ingredients]] | "See all" |
| 3 | Paneer | [[paneer]] | A dossier |
| 4 | Milk → Paneer | [[milk-to-paneer]] | "Make paneer at home" |
| 5 | Palak paneer | [[palak-paneer]] | The recipe |

## Exit
A recipe with numbered steps, open while cooking.

## Escapes
The nav bar is on every screen: Ingredients, Pairings, Cookbook, and the logo
back to Home. A dossier also opens the recipe directly, skipping step 4.
