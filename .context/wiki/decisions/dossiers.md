# The site is ingredient dossiers

**Choice:** The site is organised around ingredient dossiers and pairings, not journeys. Nav: Ingredients, Pairings, Cookbook. A dossier is a single-frame bento of tiles, not a page of chapters; only [[tomato]] (Grow, Source, Storage, Cook, Pairs with / Avoid with) and [[grow-tomato]] are drawn this way so far. See [[ingredient-dossier]].

**Why:** the chapter version was judged too text-dense; Origin and In the body were dropped on the owner's feedback (commits `4067af7`, `b507bd8`).

**Rejected:** a transformation-graph model with journeys as the whole site · seven text chapters per ingredient. [[skills]] has no way in; [[journeys]] is reached from the footer only.
